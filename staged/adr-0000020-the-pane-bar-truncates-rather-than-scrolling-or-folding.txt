# 0000020 The pane bar truncates rather than scrolling or folding

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-05
- Supersedes: nothing. It settles question 7 in `QUESTIONS.md`

## Context

Question 7 asked whether the pane bar scrolls or folds when there are more
panes than it has columns for, and named the risk plainly: a reader with more
panes than the bar can show is a reader who cannot see the pane they want.

The reader answered that the bar does neither. That settles what it does not
do without yet saying what happens in the case the question was about, so a
second question followed: what does happen when the bar is too narrow for the
panes open. The reader's answer there is that it truncates silently.

## Decision

**The pane bar neither scrolls nor folds.** It has no independent scroll
state and no windowing around the focused pane.

**It truncates.** When there are more panes than the bar has columns for, it
shows as many pane numbers as fit, left to right, and nothing marks that more
exist past the edge.

## Reasoning

No reasoning is recorded for why truncation was chosen over scrolling,
folding, or wrapping to a second row. The reader ratified the overflow
behaviour directly rather than this record arguing for it, and that is named
here rather than a justification being supplied after the fact.

## Consequences

**A pane past the edge is invisible in the bar**, including the focused one
if focus moves there by `/pane next`, `/pane previous`, or `/pane N`. The bar
gives no indicator, so a reader cannot tell from the bar alone that panes
exist beyond what it shows.

**Switching to a pane does not bring it into view in the bar.** Since the bar
does not fold around focus, moving focus to a truncated pane changes which
pane is live without changing what the bar displays.

**The reader finds an off-bar pane some other way**, such as `/pane next`
stepping to it blind or a count kept elsewhere. Nothing in this record
provides that way; it is a consequence left for whatever reads pane state
next.

## Alternatives considered

Folding the bar around the focused pane, so the current pane is always
visible within a bounded window. Rejected by the reader without a reason
recorded.

Wrapping the bar to a second terminal row when panes exceed one row's width.
Not raised as a choice; truncation was given directly.

## What this leaves open

How a reader finds a pane that the bar has truncated off the edge. Not asked
by question 7 and not answered here.

## Notes

Nothing in the tree has changed. There is no pane bar in the tree.
