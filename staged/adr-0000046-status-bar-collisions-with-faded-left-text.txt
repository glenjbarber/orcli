# 0000046 Status-bar collisions with faded left text

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-10-07
- Supersedes: nothing

## Context

The accepted UI redesign leaves detailed status-bar behavior open. The status bar has two halves, and their items can run out of room when the terminal narrows. A prior TODO proposed dropping fields in a priority order. Glen rejected that proposal and specified overlap with a visual indication of which text is behind.

## Decision

**When the two halves of status bar 2 collide for space, their items overlap.** They are not dropped according to a shed order.

**Text on the left-most items visibly fades in font color to indicate that it is "behind" the other half.**

This record establishes the collision and fading behavior only. Field membership and order remain separate design details.

## Consequences

Status-bar items remain present in a narrow layout. The fading treatment indicates which text is behind the other half when the halves overlap. The earlier field-shed proposal is not authoritative.

This proposal records the design decision only. It does not claim implementation.
