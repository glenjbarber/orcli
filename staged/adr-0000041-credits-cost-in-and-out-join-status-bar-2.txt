# 0000041 Credits, Cost, In and Out join status bar 2

- Status: accepted, and SUPERCEDED by `UI-redesign.md`, which redefines the screen regions, status-bar fields, and prompt this record describes
- Date: 2026-10-06
- Supersedes: nothing. It extends 0000021, which named status bar 2's
  fields, and 0000016 and 0000037, which placed the shed ladder

## Context

The reference `openrouter-cli` top bar carries Credits, Cost, tokens In and
tokens Out, the figures that move slowly across a session. `DESIGN.md` and
0000021 carried the redesign's two bars forward from the reference without
a place for any of the four. Status bar 2, `Hostname | Context | Autosave |
Stealth | Approval`, is where the rest of that slow-moving group already
sits.

## Decision

**Status bar 2 gains four fields: Credits, Cost, In and Out.** The order,
slowest-changing toward the edge:

```
Hostname | Credits | Cost | Context | In | Out | Autosave | Stealth | Approval
```

**The shed ladder for bar 2 (0000016, 0000037) gains one step.** The existing
order sheds Approval, then Stealth, then Autosave, then Context, then the
bar drops whole. Credits, Cost, In and Out shed together, as one step,
between Approval and Stealth:

```
Approval → Credits/Cost/In/Out (together) → Stealth → Autosave → Context → bar drops whole
```

## Reasoning

All four are the reference's own slow-moving group, the same group Hostname
and Context already belong to, so they join the bar that already carries
those two rather than status bar 1, which carries Provider, Model, Status
and Verbosity, the fields that change on the fly next to what the reader
is typing.

The four shed together rather than one at a time because they are one
reading, the session's running cost and usage, and a bar that dropped Cost
alone while keeping Credits, In and Out would show a partial figure that
reads as a different thing than what it is.

They shed before Stealth and Autosave because those two are settings the
reader chose, not figures that change on their own, and a setting is worth
more screen space than a running total once space is short. They shed
after Approval because 0000016 already placed Approval first and nothing
here argues to move it.

## Consequences

A reader at a narrow terminal loses the running cost and usage figures
before losing Stealth, Autosave or Context. The cost ledger itself is
unaffected; this is a display question only.

## Alternatives considered

Splitting the four across both bars, by fast- versus slow-moving, was not
chosen: all four come from the same source and change at the same rate, so
splitting them would separate one reading into two places for no reason
tied to how often either half changes.

## What this leaves open

Nothing from this question. The shed ladder for bar 1 is unaffected.
