## Reading order

0001 and 0002 are the frame that is being replaced and go together. 0010
supersedes both in what it describes, and both stand as records of a decision
that was taken and then replaced.

0003 and 0004 are the connection and the model. 0004 refers back to 0003
because a probe and a move are different acts. Both describe behaviour in the
tree and neither is affected by the redesign.

0005 is a proposal about the external library and is the only record of the
first five that describes something not in the tree. Its line counts measure
the frame this redesign replaces rather than a plan.

0006 stands. 0007 stands for a session per pane, pane 0 as main, `Ctrl+B` as a
non-latching prefix, program-owned scrollback, and a session log that survives
a crash. 0008 and 0009 are superseded by 0010, and both stand as records of a
decision that was taken and then replaced.

0010 is the API. It answers the questions 0007 and 0008 left open, it gives
the screen in full, and where it is silent a question is still open and is
listed at its end.

0011 fills the one gap 0010 left in the keys a reader touches, and it is read
with 0010 rather than after it.

## What is superseded, and where it went

The frame in the tree is described by 0001 and 0002. 0010 replaces it.

`staged/status-interrupt.txt` made escape an unqualified interrupt that sends
nothing. 0006 supersedes it.

The nine-row footer that `staged/status-*.txt` records describe existed only in
uncommitted work in a worktree that was removed. It is not in the tree and
nothing supersedes it.

The prompt was `root@localhost $ ` in 0009 and is `root@lolhost @ ` in 0010.
The reader wrote both and marked the second as deliberate.

## What these records are not

They are not a specification. `DESIGN.md` describes the interface being built,
and these records say why it is that way rather than what it does. 0010 and
0011 together are the closest thing to a specification here, and they are still
records of decisions.

They are not exhaustive. Smaller decisions live in the comments on the code
that carries them.

They are not rewritten when the code changes. A record that is edited to agree
with the code is no longer a record of what was decided, and the thing worth
keeping is the rejected alternatives and the corrections. Five records here
carry a correction or a replacement rather than a deletion: 0005 keeps the
belief its first draft held about the clipboard, 0006 keeps the escape rule it
replaced, 0008 keeps the tiling it rejected, 0009 keeps the prompt 0010
replaced, and 0010 keeps the keys it did not mention.
