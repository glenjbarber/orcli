# Design: panes overlap right-over-left instead of shedding

Replaces the shed ladder's drop-a-region behavior
(`staged/adr-0000016-the-shed-ladder.txt`) for a short terminal: instead of a
pane losing a region outright, the right pane draws over the left, and the
left pane's text steps down in brightness rather than being clipped.

This also bears on the pane bar records
(`staged/adr-0000019-the-pane-bar-returns.txt`,
`staged/adr-0000020-the-pane-bar-truncates-rather-than-scrolling-or-folding.txt`),
which describe panes keeping separate, non-overlapping space. Overlap only
makes sense once panes share columns, which those records do not describe
today.

## What it is for

The shed ladder's answer to "not enough room" is to remove a region. A reader
loses the evidence that the region exists at all, not just its current
content. Overlap keeps the region present but dimmed, so a reader scanning a
short terminal sees that something is there and partly obscured by the
focused pane, rather than seeing nothing and having to remember it is there.

## The mechanic

The rightmost pane - the focused one - draws on top, at full brightness. A
pane to its left draws underneath it rather than being shed; where the two
overlap, the left pane's text is visible but dimmed, and the dimming steps
down further for each pane further back in the stack.

## Open questions

Raised here rather than settled, since none of them is this draft's to
decide alone:

- How many brightness steps, and what the floor is. A pane dimmed all the
  way to invisible is shedding again under a different name, which defeats
  the reason for choosing overlap over the ladder in the first place.
- Whether an overlapped, dimmed pane answers scroll and copy while obscured,
  or only once it is brought to the front and loses the dimming.
- Whether this replaces the shed ladder outright, or only applies once the
  pane bar (0000019/0000020) is itself decided to stay, since overlap
  presumes panes occupy the same columns and the pane bar records as written
  do not.
- `UI-redesign.md`'s screen (full-screen terminal, two bottom status bars)
  names no pane bar at all. If the pane bar is cut following that document,
  this draft needs to be re-scoped against whatever replaces panes-as-columns
  rather than against the shed ladder as it stands.
